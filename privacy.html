Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Expozia is a film light meter for iOS. This policy explains what the app does — and doesn't do — with your data. The short version: Expozia runs on your device and does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information with me or any third party.

Camera and LiDAR

Expozia uses your iPhone's camera to measure light and, on supported devices, the LiDAR sensor to measure distance for flash calculations. This data is processed live on your device to produce exposure readings and, if you turn on exposure simulation, to preview how your settings will expose the shot. The app does not record, save, or transmit the camera feed or depth data — a photo is saved only when you take one.

Photo Library access

Expozia requests full read/write access to your Photos library. This is used to create and manage a dedicated "Expozia" album (with a subalbum per film roll), save the photos you capture there, let you browse them from the in-app gallery, and remove them if you delete a shot or a roll from the app. Expozia does not read, browse, or modify photos outside the albums it created — the one exception is "Add Scan": if you attach a scanned negative to a roll, you pick it yourself through Apple's system photo picker, and only that one photo is added to the roll's album. The photo itself stays where it is in your library (it is not duplicated or modified), and removing it from a roll in Expozia only removes it from that album — the original is never deleted. Nothing else in your library is accessed.

Your logbook

Rolls, frames, exposure settings, and any photos you choose to save are stored locally on your device and in your Photos library. To keep scans you've attached to rolls linked across reinstalls and your devices, Expozia stores a small list of those links in your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage. This data is handled by Apple under your iCloud settings and is never sent to me or to any third party.

Sharing

When you tap Share on a photo, Expozia hands that photo to Apple's system share sheet. Where it goes next is up to you and the app you choose; Expozia doesn't send it anywhere on its own.

Data I collect

None. Expozia has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or tracking. I don't receive any data about you or how you use the app.

Third parties

Expozia does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. Purchases are handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy terms; I never see your payment details.

Children

Expozia is a general-purpose photography tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
